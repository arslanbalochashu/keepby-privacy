KeepBy Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Information stored on your device

KeepBy stores the item names, store names, dates, notes, and return and refund statuses that you enter. The app keeps these records in private storage on your Android device. KeepBy does not transmit your records to the developer or collect personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or cloud sync.

Optional calendar handoff

If you tap Add to calendar, KeepBy passes the item name and selected date to your calendar app so that you can review and save an event. KeepBy does not read your calendar. Your calendar app may sync the event according to its own settings and privacy policy. Editing a record in KeepBy does not update an event you already saved.

Retention and deletion

Records stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual returns, clear all entries in the app's Privacy screen, or uninstall KeepBy. Delete any calendar events separately in your calendar app.
